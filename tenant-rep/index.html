Common Questions

Answers Before You Ask

How exactly does the landlord pay your fee?
In commercial real estate, the landlord's broker and the tenant's broker each earn a commission — typically 3–6% of the total lease value — paid by the landlord. This is standard practice built into every commercial deal. It doesn't change your rent, and it doesn't cost you anything extra. The landlord expects to pay it whether you have a broker or not.
Are you steering me toward certain properties?
No. My commission comes from whichever landlord owns the space you ultimately lease — so I have no incentive to push you toward any specific property. My only interest is finding you the right fit. I search the entire market, including off-market spaces that never get publicly listed.
How long does a typical search take?
Most searches take 4–12 weeks from first conversation to signed lease, depending on how specific your requirements are and how quickly we identify the right space. LA has a lot of inventory — the process is finding the right fit, not just the first available option.
What if I've already found a space I like?
I can still help. Bring me in before you sign anything. I'll review the lease terms, flag hidden costs like CAM charges and escalations, and negotiate better conditions — often saving significantly more than my commission costs. It's one of the highest-ROI moves a tenant can make.
