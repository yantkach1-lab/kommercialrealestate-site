Frequently Asked Questions

What Are the Requirements to Work with You?

We work with property owners, investors, and businesses of all sizes. Whether you're acquiring, leasing, or optimizing a property, here’s what we require:

  • A clear understanding of your goals and vision. If you do not have clear goals an intuitive feeling is enough to begin exploring.
  • Commitment to collaborative planning and open communication, with an understanding that you will need to put in effort on your end to make things successful.
  • Willingness to invest in tailored strategies to maximize your property’s potential, starting at $20K+.

For specialized services, such as Build-Out Planning or Acquisition Consulting, we may request additional details about your property or investment goals. To get started, simply apply, and if you’re a fit, we’ll schedule a call to discuss your project in greater detail.

How Long Does It Take?

Every project is unique, so timelines vary depending on the scope and complexity. If you apply and are a good fit, we’ll provide a customized timeline based on your specific goals.

Our focus is on delivering quality over speed. While we work efficiently to get you results as quickly as possible, we treat our work as a craft. If your main priority is speed alone, KRE may not be the best option for you.

So How Does It Work?

Our process is seamless, strategic, and uniquely tailored to your needs—every engagement is one of a kind. Apply and see if you are the right fit.

Is It Virtual or In Person?

Most of the work we do is virtual, ensuring convenience and flexibility. However, we also host quarterly gatherings where our clients and community members can connect, collaborate, and grow together.

Certain projects may require in-person meetings depending on the scope of work. In such cases, the specifics of the project will determine whether an in-person meeting is necessary. We let the work do the talk and we listen to what the work needs.

How Many Clients Do You Work with at One Time?

Our client roster is intentionally kept small and exclusive. We take on a limited number of projects at any given time because of the highly collaborative and tailored nature of our work.

We prioritize finding clients who align with our vibe and approach to the work. This ensures we have the capacity, attention, and resources to deliver exceptional results for every engagement. We focus on the craft and results.
